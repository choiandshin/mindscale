Prospective students

Please include in your first email: a paragraph on what interests you about educational measurement, your CV or transcript, and any experience with statistics, R or Python, or assessment work.

Applications to Sogang's graduate programs are handled by the university; contacting the lab first is welcome and helps us tell you whether the fit is right before you invest in an application.

Collaboration

We collaborate with schools, testing organizations, and research groups on automated scoring, adaptive test design, automatic item generation, and secondary analysis of large-scale assessment data. Email is the fastest way to start that conversation.
